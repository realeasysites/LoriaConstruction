# Instant Estimate: what I need from you

Takes about 15 minutes, and you can text me the answers. You can change any of these numbers yourself later in your dashboard.

## 1. Your price per square foot (installed, all-in)
Include base, forms, rebar/mesh, concrete, labor, and finishing.
- [ ] Broom finish: $____ /sq ft
- [ ] Exposed aggregate: $____ /sq ft (or "don't offer")
- [ ] Stamped: $____ /sq ft
- [ ] Stamped + colored: $____ /sq ft
- [ ] Any finish I'm missing, or one you'd rather not advertise?

## 2. Tear-out
- [ ] Removing an existing patio, pavers, or slab: $____ /sq ft

## 3. Add-ons
- [ ] Steps: $____ each
- [ ] Seat wall: $____ per linear foot (or "don't do them")
- [ ] Fire pit pad: $____ flat
- [ ] Anything else people ask for often? (walkways, sealing, drainage, heated, etc.)

## 4. Hard sites
How much more do you charge when…
- [ ] It's a moderate carry or there's a gate? ____% more
- [ ] It's tight, a backyard, or needs a wheelbarrow or pump? ____% more
- [ ] There's a gentle slope? ____% more
- [ ] There's a real slope or hill? ____% more

## 5. The filter
- [ ] Minimum job, the smallest job worth your truck: $____
- [ ] Should the range lean high so nobody is surprised at the visit? (Default: 10% under to 15% over your number.)
- [ ] Largest patio the form should price before it says "call us": ____ sq ft
- [ ] Is it OK to ask "what were you hoping to spend?" (It's optional for the customer.)

## 6. Where it goes
- [ ] Which email should get the lead alerts?
- [ ] Which phone number should show on the estimate?
- [ ] Towns or areas you serve, and any you don't want leads from?
- [ ] Anything to add to the "this is not a quote" wording?

## 7. The switchover
- [ ] When does your CreteQuotes plan renew? We'll run both side by side for a couple of weeks, then cancel it.
