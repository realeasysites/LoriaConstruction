The Loria signature

The look of natural stone. The strength of poured concrete.

Stamped concrete is what we're known for. Each slab is poured, colored, stamped by hand and sealed, so you get the look of slate, stone or wood plank as one solid surface that won't shift like pavers.

  • Your pattern, your color. Ashlar slate, random stone, wood plank and more, in warm tans, cool grays or deep charcoals with antiqued highlights.
  • Contrasting borders. A stamped border in a second color frames the patio and makes curves stand out.
  • Custom medallions. Compass roses and inlays that turn a patio into a centerpiece.
  • Sealed to last. A finishing seal brings out the color and protects the surface.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Don't see yours? Send us a message or call (585) 727-8149.

How much is an estimate?

Free. We come to your home or business in person so every measurement is right and we can give you an accurate estimate and answer your questions. Want a number before the visit? Use the Instant Estimate for a ballpark.

Is the Instant Estimate a quote?

No. It's an honest ballpark range based on your answers. The real price comes from the free on-site visit, where we check access, grade, drainage and your exact layout.

Is Loria Construction insured?

Yes, fully insured. We always have proof of insurance on site while working and can email it to you too.

How far do you travel?

Typically within 30 miles of Rochester, NY. We're willing to travel further for larger jobs.

Will my property be left clean?

We clean up as best we can after each day of work and restore everything to the best of our abilities when the job is done.

What kinds of projects do you do?

Stamped and decorative concrete, patios, pool decks, sidewalks, driveways, walkways, steps, brick, block, stone veneer, fireplaces, chimneys, additions, garage floors, foundations, retaining walls and tile.

Do you repair chimneys?

Yes. We do all types of chimney repairs, including tuck pointing, chimney capping and full replacement.

Why invest in masonry work?

Updated masonry can increase the value of your home or business and adds structural durability. It also makes your property look better.
